# 121 to Win

A Bulgarian election simulator. Build your candidate, raise your banner, hit the trail, and fight for a 121-seat majority — or form a coalition.

## How to play

- **Online:** play it at <https://bdpesho.github.io/BG-Election-Simulator/>
- **Offline:** download the repo and open `index.html` in any browser.

20 weeks, 29 electoral districts, and a strict 4% threshold. Dozens of rivals want your voters. Good luck.

## Game modes

- **Parliamentary Campaign** — 20 weeks, 29 districts, a 4% threshold and 240 seats. Win 121 or build a coalition.
- **Presidential Race** — the 25 October 2026 election. Take over one of the six real tickets (with their late-September polling) or run your own candidate with a running mate. Six weeks to round one; a candidate only wins outright with more than 50% *and* turnout above 50%, otherwise the top two meet in a runoff on 1 November.
